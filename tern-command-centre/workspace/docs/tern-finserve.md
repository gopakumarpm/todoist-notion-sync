# Tern Finserve — charter

**Mandate.** Loan distributor (DSA): HL, PL, BL, LAP. Sources, qualifies and files applications with banks and NBFCs; earns commission from the lender on disbursal. Does not lend or guarantee approvals.

**Entity.** eSource Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. (also carries recruitment). Separate from CredSuvidha.

**Launch.** Pune first, then PAN India. Phase 2: Auto, Education, Gold loans.

## Rules that never bend
1. Distributor, not lender; always disclose the lending partner.
2. No guaranteed approvals, rates or timelines.
3. No borrower fee before disbursal; commission is paid by the lender.
4. Consent before bureau pulls or data sharing (DPDP).
5. Documents are genuine; suspected fraud is escalated, never worked around.
6. Suitability over commission.
7. Grievances logged within one working day.
8. Regulatory standing is verified before the panel goes live.
