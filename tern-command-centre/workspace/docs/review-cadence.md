# Review cadence

## Weekly Group Review — Monday 08:00 IST
Chaired by Max. One report to Gopakumar in a fixed order:

1. Headline — three lines Gopakumar must know
2. Skills review — every agent Fit / Stretch / Gap → Keep / Update / Upgrade / Absorb / Hire; upgrades made this week
3. Utilization — per company and per person
4. Tasks completed
5. Tasks ongoing (owner, due date, blocker)
6. Recurring tasks — ran / missed
7. Skill upgrades and new skills installed in tern-os
8. New hires (with reuse-before-hire check)
9. Promotions and transfers
10. New ventures and pipeline
11. Financial report — company P&Ls, intercompany charging, tool spend
12. Family services — Academy, Animal Health, Family Health Desk
13. Risks, compliance and safety-rule adherence
14. Decisions needed from Gopakumar
15. Next week's plan

## Other rhythms
- **Weekly:** WBR per Taurus site; HDFC Ergo reviews Tue & Fri 15:00; Family Health Desk check-in; Aarshi's study plan (Saturday/Sunday); Pluto & Bannu check (Saturday).
- **Monthly:** CEO business reviews with Max; family reviews (Aarshi, pets, family health) with Gopakumar.
- **Quarterly:** group review with Gopakumar; MedTech regulatory & pipeline review.
