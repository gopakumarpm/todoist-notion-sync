# Live map

The Live panel of the command centre. Districts for the Corporate Centre and the four companies; agents bob in place; packets travel the curves of every recorded run (green done, blue running); cron rings tick on their owners (red when a schedule needs attention); gold tickets count tasks in "doing". A ticker cycles through everything that has happened across runs. Works at phone width (2×2 districts) and respects reduced-motion settings.
