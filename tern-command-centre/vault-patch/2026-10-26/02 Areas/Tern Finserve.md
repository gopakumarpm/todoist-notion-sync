# 💳 Tern Finserve

Tern Group's **sixth operating company**, decided by you on **12 Oct 2026**: a company for financing — home loans (HL), personal loans (PL), business loans (BL) and loan against property (LAP). Built as a **loan distributor (DSA)**, not a lender: it sources, qualifies and files applications with banks and NBFCs and earns commission from the lender on disbursal. No balance sheet, no guaranteed approvals.

- **CEO:** Meera Kulkarni → reports to Max (Group MD)
- **Legal entity:** eSource Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. (the same entity that carries [[02 Areas/eSource|eSource]]); separate from [[Projects/CredSuvidha|CredSuvidha]]
- **Launch:** Pune first, then PAN India state by state. Phase 2: Auto, Education and Gold loans
- **Headcount:** 6 (3 F / 3 M), all `paper` until their agent files exist

## 🔁 The journey
Lead → consent + KYC → eligibility screen (Arvind) → lender match (Arvind, Elena) → login and documents (Pooja) → sanction → disbursal → commission reconciliation (Pooja → Priya) → care and grievance close-out (Thomas).

## 🛡️ Rules that never bend
1. Distributor, not lender; always disclose the lending partner.
2. No guaranteed approvals, rates or timelines.
3. No borrower fee before disbursal; commission is paid by the lender.
4. Consent before bureau pulls or data sharing (DPDP).
5. Documents are genuine; suspected fraud is escalated, never worked around.
6. Suitability over commission.
7. Grievances acknowledged and logged within one working day; published escalation path.
8. Regulatory standing verified before the lender panel goes live.
